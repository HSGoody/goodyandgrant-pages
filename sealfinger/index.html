Sam Applewhite crime series

She doesn’t go looking for crimes.

Sam Applewhite is a security consultant with a job that takes her into other people’s lives. When an elderly client disappears and nobody else seems to care, Sam can’t walk away.

She is not a detective. She is the person who notices when something is wrong — and keeps getting dragged into the danger everyone else missed.
