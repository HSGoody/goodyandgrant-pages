Write ThatNovel

An eight-week practical course for people who are ready to turn an idea, a half-started manuscript or a long-held ambition into a novel.

Apply for the Autumn course

What's in the course

The material we'll cover

  • Finding and pressure-testing your idea
  • Genre, readers and the book market
  • Hooks, openings and first chapters
  • Character, motivation, wants and needs
  • Plot, structure and story questions
  • Conflict, tension and stakes
  • Point of view and narrative distance
  • Voice, style and tone
  • Dialogue, scenes and pace
  • Setting, atmosphere and sensory writing
  • Writing habits, critique and feedback
  • Revision, finishing and publishing

Your tutors

Writers who know the work

The course will be led by professional novelists Heide Goody and Iain Grant, who have been making their living as published authors for several years. They bring practical experience of developing ideas, solving structural problems, finishing manuscripts, working with editors and getting books into readers' hands. Just as importantly, they understand that every writer and every novel is different. Their aim is not to hand out rigid rules, but to show you what works, explain why it works and help you make useful decisions about your own book.
